# Dataset policy and responsible reporting

- The dataset source is a third-party community-hosted Products-10K variant. This repository does not mirror or redistribute the images.
- Review the original dataset's licensing and terms before broader publication or use.
- Image content, model checkpoints, credentials, virtual environments, downloaded paper PDFs and temporary caches are excluded.
- SKU examples include fashion accessories, cosmetics and handbags; do **not** describe the pilot as packaged grocery recognition.
- Only **6 official test samples per retained SKU**. Report uncertainty and discuss limited generalizability.
- All reported results are based on **validation**, not the untouched official test set.
- Exact pixel-hash deduplication does not ensure absence of perceptually identical images or correlated capture conditions.
- Final research conclusions require independent human label review and a pre-specified final evaluation.
